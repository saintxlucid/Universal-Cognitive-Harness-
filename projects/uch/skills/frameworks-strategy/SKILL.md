# Strategy Frameworks

Strategy decides the destination and the basis for success; planning designs
the route. The wheel is a continuous inquiry system, not a planning document.

## When to Use

- Organizational alignment and strategic review
- Competitive positioning questions
- Distinguishing direction (strategy) from execution (plan)

## Strategy Wheel (MCP `strategy`)

Four quadrants, five questions each — 20 total:

1. **Purpose & Direction (WHY)**: existence, mission, beliefs, 3-year vision,
   unconstrained success
2. **Market & Advantage (WHO)**: highest-value customer, daily pain, industry
   change, why chosen over competitors, hard-to-copy offers
3. **Goals & Metrics (WHAT)**: top 3 priorities, quarterly success, the one
   number, review cadence, winning milestone
4. **Actions & Tactics (HOW)**: review rhythm, quick wins, blockers,
   ownership, 90-day deliverables

The wheel cycles: purpose → market → goals → actions → results → review →
purpose.

## Strategy vs Plan

- **Strategy** (3-5 years): purpose, arena, advantage, capabilities, revenue
  logic — answers *why* and *where*
- **Plan** (days-weeks-months): goals, ownership, resources, timeline,
  tracking — answers *how*, *who*, *when*

Common failures: chasing too many goals, ignoring competition, treating
strategy as one-time work, activity over outcomes, no accountability,
ignoring feedback.
