# Deep Research Skill

## Core Principle

Never generate content based solely on general knowledge. A single search query is NEVER enough.

## Phase 1: Broad Exploration

1. Initial survey: search the main topic for overall context.
2. Identify dimensions: extract key subtopics, themes, angles, and viewpoints.
3. Map the territory: note different perspectives and stakeholders.

## Phase 2: Deep Dive

For each important dimension:

1. Specific queries with precise keywords per subtopic.
2. Multiple phrasings of each query.
3. Fetch full content of important sources â€” not just snippets.
4. Follow references to other important sources.

## Phase 3: Diversity & Validation

Seek diverse information types:

| Type | Purpose | Query hints |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Facts & Data | Concrete evidence | "statistics", "data", "market size" |
| Examples & Cases | Real-world applications | "case study", "implementation" |
| Expert Opinions | Authority perspectives | "expert analysis", "interview" |
| Trends & Predictions | Future direction | "trends", "forecast", "future of" |
| Comparisons | Context and alternatives | "vs", "comparison", "alternatives" |
| Challenges & Criticisms | Balanced view | "challenges", "limitations", "criticism" |

## Phase 4: Synthesis Check

Before proceeding, verify:

- [ ] Searched from at least 3-5 different angles
- [ ] Fetched and read at least the top 2-3 sources in full
- [ ] Included diverse information types (data, opinions, trends)
- [ ] Identified contradictions or debates between sources
- [ ] Can cite specific sources for every claim
